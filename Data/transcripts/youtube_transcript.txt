--- Title: 🧠 Zuckerberg: DON&#39;T just start a company!                      #technology #business #tech #startup ---

The most important thing that entrepreneurs should do is pick something they care about, work on it, but don't actually commit to turning it into a company until it's working. If you look at the data of the very best companies that have gotten built, a tremendous percent of them have been built that way, and not from people who decided up front that they wanted to start a company.
