Licensing Restrictions
Due to licensing restrictions, Harvard Business School Case Studies are restricted to classroom use by individual students.
The BU Libraries, including Pardee Library, are unable to purchase these cases for the library’s collections or obtain them through Interlibrary Loan.
Individuals who wish to acquire copies of these cases for personal use can purchase them directly from Harvard Business Publishing (see link below).
Harvard Business Publishing: Cases
Instructors who wish to assign Harvard Business School cases to their students can arrange course packs directly with Harvard Business Publishing (see link below). Students must pay a fee to access these course packs, with costs varying depending on the number of cases included.
Harvard Business Publishing: Coursepacks
If you prefer not to pay a fee for cases, please explore this guide further for information on cases available through existing library resources or Open Access Cases (free).
Harvard Business Review
Harvard Business Review via Business Source Complete
A selection of case studies from Harvard Business Review from 1922-present can be accessed via the Business Source Complete database. To limit your search results to case studies, select "Case study" for the Document Type.
Harvard Business Review via Gale Academic Onefile
Full-text coverage of Harvard Business Review articles from January 1, 1997 to May 1, 2000.
Harvard Business Review Digital Articles via Business Source Complete
Contains select items from HBR from 2007-present. Select "Case study" for the Document Type to limit search results.
Please note that since August 2013, Harvard Business Review has designated 500 of their most popular articles as "read-only," restricting the ability to print or download these articles via Business Source Complete. You can view the original list of these articles at the link below. If you require an article from the HBR 500 list, you may be able to access it through our print subscription of Harvard Business Review at Pardee Library.
Harvard Business Review Top 500

Online Resources
Once you have identified a case study that you wish to analyze, the sources listed below can help you analyze the case materials.
Guide to case analysis
From the publisher McGraw Hill. Includes sections on objectives of case analysis, preparing a case for class discussion, preparing a written case analysis and the Ten Commandments of Case Analysis.
Cengage Learning - Case Studies
Explains how to effectively analyze cases and write a case study analysis. Provides a checklist and explanation of areas to consider, suggested research tools, and tips on financial analysis.
How to Analyze a Case Study
From the Simmons University Writing Center.
Writing a Case Analysis
From the University of New South Wales Business School.
Writing a Case Study Analysis
From The University of Arizona, Global Campus, Writing Center.
Books
The Case Study Handbook: a Student's Guide, Revised Edition
by
William Ellet
Location: Online
Publication Date: 2018
William Ellet presents a potent new approach for efficiently analyzing, discussing, and writing about cases. Strategies and templates, in addition to several sample Harvard Business School cases, help you apply the author's framework.
Learning with Cases
by
Louise A. Mauffette-Leenders; James A. Erskine; Michiel R. Leenders
Location: Online & Pardee Library HF1111 .M38 1997
Publication Date: 1997
This book is a concise handbook written specifically for students to enhance their learning with cases.
Conducting Case Study Research for Business and Management Students
by
Marilyn Taylor; Mikael Søndergaard; Bill Lee; Mark N. K. Saunders
Location: Pardee Stacks HD30.4 .L44 2017
Publication Date: 2018
Bill Lee and Mark Saunders describe the properties of case study designs in organizational research, exploring the uses, advantages and limitations of case research.
Doing Case Study Research
by
Bob Algozzine Hancock
Location: Online
Publication Date: 2016
Reflecting recent knowledge and developments in the field, this very practical, easy-to-use guide emphasizes learning how to do case study research--from the first step of deciding whether a case study is the way to go to the last step of verifying and confirming findings before disseminating them.
The Anatomy of the Case Study
by
Gary Thomas; Kevin Myers
Location: Online
Publication Date: 2015
This sharp, stimulating title explores the historical, theoretical and practical bones of modern case study research, offering to social scientists a framework for understanding and working with this form of inquiry.
Art of Case Analysis
by
Robert C. Ronstadt
Location: Pardee Library HF1118 .R66 1993
Publication Date: 1980
A comprehensive treatment for how to read, prepare, discuss, write analyses of, and record findings associated with cases or other assignments that involve interactive learning.
Case Study Method: key issues, key texts
by
Roger Gomm (Editor); Martyn Hammersley (Editor); Peter Foster (Editor)
Location: Online
Publication Date: 2000
This is the most comprehensive guide to the current uses and importance of case study methods in social research. The editors bring together key contributions from the field which reflect different interpretations of the purpose and capacity of case study research.
