Frequently Asked Questions
What support can I offer my students around analyzing cases and preparing for discussion?
Case discussions can be a big departure from the norm for students who are used to lecture-based classes. The
Case Analysis Coach
is an interactive tutorial on reading and analyzing a case study. The
Case Study Handbook
covers key skills students need to read, understand, discuss and write about cases. The
Case Study Handbook
is also available as individual chapters
to help your students focus on specific skills.
How can I transfer my in-person case teaching plan to an online environment?
The case method can be used in an online environment without sacrificing its benefits. We have compiled a few resources to help you create transformative online learning experiences with the case method. Learn how HBS brought the case method online in this
podcast
, gather some quick guidance from the article "
How to Teach Any Case Online
", review the
Teaching Cases Online Guide
for a deep dive, and check out our
Teaching Online Resources Page
for more insights and inspiration.

Fundamentals of Case Teaching is an online, self-paced introduction to teaching with the case method. It is ideal for faculty who are new to case teaching or who want to learn practical skills for using case studies in their classrooms. The course is organized into six modules that coach educators through the essentials, including establishing classroom roles and norms, opening the discussion, orchestrating the discussion flow, planning and creating the classroom experience, navigating common challenges with case teaching, and evaluating students. The course includes videos of the case method in action (Harvard Business School cases are used as examples), as well as insights and commentary from experienced case method instructors. Educators will also practice and receive feedback with interactive, scenario-based exercises. Fundamentals of Case Teaching will prepare you for the challenges and opportunities of case discussions in any classroom and at any course level.
Purchase includes one year of access to the course. Total seat time is approximately six hours but can be completed at your own pace and on your own schedule. Learning Objectives
• Learn about the value of the case method and the roles and expectations of the instructor and student.
• Learn best practices for opening a case discussion, including crafting good questions.
• Understand how to facilitate the flow of case discussion, including questioning, listening, and responding to students, and managing student participation.
• Learn how to set up the classroom environment for a case discussion, including planning a lesson and using classroom space effectively.
• Understand how to navigate common challenges with case teaching, including addressing unwelcome behaviors, handling sensitive topics, and turning challenges into opportunities.
• Learn how to evaluate student participation.
